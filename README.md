# HJ-Andrews-WY2324
This dataset contains hydrologic, biogeochemical, and dissolved oxygen observations collected in Watershed 1 (WS01) at the H.J. Andrews Experimental Forest, Oregon, USA, as part of a study examining dissolved oxygen dynamics during seasonal streamflow recession and intermittency. Data were collected primarily during water years 2024-2025 across a network of sites spanning a 340-m section of the stream beginning at the gage house. The dataset includes high-frequency dissolved oxygen and water temperature time series, discrete grab-sample measurements of stream chemistry and related environmental variables, and stream surveys documenting spatial and hydrologic conditions across the study reach. Together, these data characterize changes in dissolved oxygen and potential environmental controls as the stream transitions from connected flow to spatially intermittent conditions and subsequently reconnects. The dataset supports analyses of spatial and temporal variability in dissolved oxygen and its relationships with hydrology, temperature, light, groundwater influence, and stream chemistry.
